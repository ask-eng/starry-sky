# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and kind, assume good intent, give and take feedback gracefully, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated. People write here in many languages and at many skill levels; please be patient with both.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form, or open an issue asking a maintainer to get in touch (don't include details in public). Reports are handled confidentially.
